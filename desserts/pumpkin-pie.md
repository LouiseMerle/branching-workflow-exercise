# Pumpkin pie recipe


## Preparation

- Remove seeds
- Bake pumpkin/squash
- Take out the inside of the pumpkin
- Use 400 grams of pumpkin pulp per pie


## Ingredients

- Pie shell
- 1/2 cup rasins
- Nutmeg
- Can of evaporated milk
- 100 grams sugar
- 0.5 tsp salt
- 2 tsp cinnamon
- 1 tsp ginger, ground or juice
- 0.5 tsp ground cloves
- 2 eggs


## Instructions

- Mix dry ingredients
- Add pumpkin pulp
- Smash manually until somehow homogeneous
- Add eggs and blend
- Add evaporated milk and blend
- Add rasins and mix


## Baking

- 15 min at 425 F
- 40-50 min at 350 F
